About Long Arc

A long partnership. A renewed catalogue.

They write with artists in mind, creating songs that can be shaped around different voices, styles and recording projects.

Long Arc Songs is the UK songwriting partnership of Phil and Jay. Their catalogue ranges from intimate acoustic songs and emotionally driven ballads to contemporary pop, classic soul and full-scale jazz-pop arrangements—all united by strong melody, direct storytelling and memorable choruses.

Previously signed to a five-year agreement with an American music publisher, their songs have been professionally produced in Nashville and put forward for artists including Michael Bublé, Westlife and Trace Adkins. After time away from actively pursuing placements, they have returned with their strongest previously unheard songs alongside new material written for today’s artists and commercial briefs.
